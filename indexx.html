Arrow Squad Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Published by KINDIX DEVS · App package: com.kindix.arrowsquad

This policy explains what information Arrow Squad (the "App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Game data stored on your device. Your progress (level, coins, heroes, skins, scenes, badges, settings and daily-reward status) is saved locally on your device. We do not create accounts and we do not upload this data to our servers.

Advertising data. The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. You can read how Google handles this at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchase data. If in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed by Google Play. We may use a purchase management service (RevenueCat) that receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status so that your purchases can be recognised. We never see or store your payment card details.

Notifications. If you turn on reminders, the App schedules notifications on your device. They are created locally and do not require us to collect personal information. You can turn them off in the App's Settings or in your device settings.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To show ads, and to measure and improve ad performance (through Google AdMob).
  • To process and restore purchases.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob and Google Play, and RevenueCat if purchases are enabled), and only as needed for them to provide their services, or when required by law.

4. Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the UK, the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.
  • Notifications: switch off reminders in the App's Settings or your device settings.
  • Your data: because game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time with "Reset progress" in Settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

6. Data retention and security

We do not keep your game data on our servers. Data held by third-party providers is retained under their own policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with a new effective date.
